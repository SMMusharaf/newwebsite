The Resort

Fourteen villas, one stretch of untouched coast

In 2019, three fishing families sold their cliffside plots to a single architect with one condition — build with the land, not over it. Vaelo Bay is the result: villas terraced into the natural rock, joined by stairways cut from the same stone.

Every room faces water. Every terrace catches the wind before it reaches the garden below.

Experiences

Days here have a shape, not a schedule

Dawn boat, the day's catch

Join the local fleet at 5:40am. Whatever comes up in the nets is on the table by lunch.

Reef swim, low tide

A shallow reef sits fifty metres off the rocks. We'll row you out and leave you to it.

Long table, cliff edge

Every Friday, one long table is set along the rock face for whoever's staying that week.

Stone-room massage

Two treatment rooms carved into the natural rock wall, cooled naturally by the stone.
